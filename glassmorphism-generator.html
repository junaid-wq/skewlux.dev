Accessibility & Readability Guidelines for Translucent Cards

      
Frosted glass panels look striking over colorful mesh gradients, but high transparency over busy photography can drop text contrast below WCAG AA standards (4.5:1 for body copy). Keep surface opacity above 0.12 on dark backgrounds, apply backdrop-filter: saturate(160%) to preserve underlying color vibrancy, and include a subtle 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2) border to define card boundaries.
