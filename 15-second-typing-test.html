How Net WPM and Accuracy Are Calculated

Standard typing benchmarks define one word as 5 keystrokes (including spaces). Net WPM divides your correctly typed characters by 5 and normalizes by elapsed minutes, penalizing uncorrected typos automatically so sloppy bursts cannot inflate your score.
