Frequently Asked Questions

Is 180 ms a good reaction time?

Yes. Scoring 180 ms or lower puts you in the top 10% of human visual reflex speeds and matches the baseline of competitive FPS players.

Why is my Spacebar score faster than my mouse click?

Mechanical and laptop scissor switches often actuate higher in the key travel than heavy mouse microswitches, and keyboard polling avoids wireless mouse sleep wake-up delays.
