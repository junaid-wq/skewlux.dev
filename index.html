What Is an Average Human Visual Reaction Time?

Across millions of desktop and laptop browser sessions, the median human visual reaction time sits between 215 ms and 250 ms. Competitive esports players using low-latency gaming mice and 240Hz+ monitors routinely score between 150 ms and 185 ms, whereas 60Hz office laptops with wireless Bluetooth mice often add 35 ms to 60 ms of hardware display and USB polling delay on top of biological nerve conduction.

How SkewLux Measures Millisecond Precision

Standard JavaScript Date.now() timers suffer from system clock drift and coarse resolution. SkewLux records stimulus paint timestamps using the high-resolution performance.now() monotonic clock synchronized with requestAnimationFrame(), isolating exact user response latency inside your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does monitor refresh rate affect reaction time scores?

Yes. A 60Hz screen refreshes once every 16.67 ms, whereas a 240Hz monitor updates every 4.17 ms—saving up to 12.5 ms before your eyes even see the green stimulus.

Can I use the Spacebar instead of clicking my mouse?

Yes. Both mouse/touch taps and the keyboard Spacebar trigger the test, allowing you to compare mechanical keyboard switch actuation against mouse microswitch latency.
