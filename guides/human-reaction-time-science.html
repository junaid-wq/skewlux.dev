When you click on a visual stimulus in a browser reaction test, total measured latency combines two distinct pipelines: biological signal transmission (retina → visual cortex → motor cortex → finger muscle contraction, roughly 140–180 ms) and hardware/OS pipeline delay (browser frame compositing + monitor scanout + mouse USB polling, roughly 15–65 ms).

Enabling Game Mode on your monitor, switching from Bluetooth to wired or 2.4GHz low-latency wireless, and running a 144Hz or 240Hz refresh rate can shave 30 ms off your measured reaction score immediately without changing your physical reflexes.
