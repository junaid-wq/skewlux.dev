How to Increase Typing Speed Past 100 WPM Without Sacrificing Accuracy

Run a live zero-latency browser benchmark right now.
Take the 100-Word Typing Test →

Every single typo costs roughly 1.5 to 2.0 seconds of rhythm because your brain must detect the error, reach for Backspace, and re-accelerate into the next bigram. Typists stuck at 65–75 WPM almost always gain more net speed by slowing down 10% to hold 98%+ accuracy than by mashing keys faster.

Focus on reading one full word ahead of the active cursor so your fingers prepare upcoming letter chords continuously rather than reacting character by character.
