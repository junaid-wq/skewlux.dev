Understanding CPS Benchmarks & USB Polling Rates

Regular single-finger clicking averages 6.0 to 7.5 CPS, while jitter-clicking and butterfly-clicking techniques reach 11 to 16+ CPS. Meanwhile, USB polling rate determines how many times per second your mouse reports sensor displacement to Windows or macOS—upgrading from 125 Hz to 1000 Hz cuts maximum cursor reporting latency from 8.0 ms down to 1.0 ms.
